## Full description (max 4000)
```
Masaya Ako is the all-in-one companion for gig-economy drivers and riders in the
Philippines. Whether you drive for Grab, Angkas, MoveIt, Joyride, FoodPanda, or on
your own, Masaya Ako helps you track your work, understand your earnings, and stay
connected with a community of fellow drivers.

WHY DRIVERS LOVE MASAYA AKO

• LIVE GPS TRACKING
Start tracking with one tap. Masaya Ako records your route, distance, and active
time, and estimates your earnings based on your own per-kilometer rate. Tracking
only runs when you choose to start it — you're always in control.

• EARNINGS AT A GLANCE
See your distance, active hours, and estimated earnings for the day. Set a daily
goal and watch your progress in real time.

• DRIVER COMMUNITY
Share tips, ask questions, and connect with other drivers in your area. Post updates,
join challenges, and see who's driving nearby on the live map.

• GROUP & PRIVATE CHAT
Message fellow drivers, coordinate with your crew, and stay in the loop with
real-time chat.

• VEHICLE MAINTENANCE REMINDERS
Keep your car, motorcycle, or bicycle in top shape with simple maintenance tracking.

• YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL
Location is collected only while you're tracking. You can share your stats with the
community or keep them private — and delete your account anytime.

Masaya Ako means "I am happy" — because driving should be rewarding, not stressful.
Download today and take control of your driving life.

Note: Masaya Ako is an independent companion app and is not affiliated with, endorsed
by, or connected to Grab, Angkas, MoveIt, Joyride, FoodPanda, or any other platform.
```
