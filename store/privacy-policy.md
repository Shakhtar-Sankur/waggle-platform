Masaya Ako ("we", "our", "the app") is operated by **[YOUR NAME / COMPANY]**,
contactable at **[YOUR EMAIL]**. This policy explains what data we collect, why,
and your rights. We comply with the **Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)**.

## 1. Information we collect

| Data | When | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Phone number | Sign up / log in | Account identity and login |
| Full name | Sign up | Displayed on your profile and community posts |
| Precise location (GPS) | **Only while you tap "Start Tracking"** | Calculate distance, earnings estimate, and optional live community map |
| Community posts & chat messages | When you post/message | To provide the community and messaging features |
| App settings (vehicle, rate, home address, goal) | When you set them | To personalize your dashboard |
| Device push token | If you enable notifications | To deliver notifications to your device |

We do **not** collect data in the background when the app is closed, and we do
**not** sell your personal data to anyone.

## 2. How location works

Location is collected **only while tracking is active** (after you tap "Start
Tracking") and stops when you tap "Stop Tracking". You control this at all times.
If you enable "Share stats with community", your approximate live position and
today's distance/earnings may be visible to other drivers on the map. You can turn
this off in Settings at any time.

## 3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored securely using **Supabase** (PostgreSQL) with row-level
security, so each user can only access their own private data. Push notifications
are delivered via **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)**. These providers process
data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.

## 4. Sharing

We share data only with:
- **Service providers** that run the app (Supabase for storage, Google Firebase for
  push, OpenStreetMap for map tiles).
- **Other users**, but only the information you choose to make public (your name,
  community posts, and — if you enable sharing — your live map presence).
- **Authorities**, only where required by law.

## 5. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may access, correct, or delete your data. The app
includes a **"Delete Account"** option that permanently removes your account and
personal data. You can also email **[YOUR EMAIL]** for any privacy request.

## 6. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your
profile, settings, messages, and location history are removed. Some anonymized or
aggregated data may be retained for analytics.

## 7. Children

Masaya Ako is intended for users **18 years and older** (working drivers). We do not
knowingly collect data from children.

## 8. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), row-level access control, and signed app
releases. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect
your data.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be noted with a new "Last updated"
date and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.
